Making a mod: POV of a begginer

October 15 2025

Hello everyone, today i want to talk about my experience qhen making a mod for a game of any kind, please consider i´m just a begginer, so i am NOT the most reliable source of information out there.

The first thing to think about is: What do you want? What do you want to create for your first mod, and for what game do you want it? Personally, I have only made mods for 2 games, these being Minecraft and Friday Night Funkin, as these offer a lot of tools to make modding easier, and fit well to my desires.

A lot of people may tell you to choose something small to begin with, i personally disagree, I think it depends on how much effort you want to put in it, however, you WILL need to balance what you want and how much time you have for it. If the idea is too excessive you might not complete it in the end. My destiny FNF mod, for instance, was initially something to broad, i wanted multiple characters, mechanics, music, everything i could add, and it forced me to delay it and delay it till it got almost cancelled entirely. For minecraft however, things were a little easier, i still got a big idea: "A dimension where you can find bosses, new creatures and structures, and a new rideable entity that flies", centered overall around moths, which are my favorite animal.

After getting the idea it's just about developing, i recommend doing it in this order: Design, animate and texture Create sounds and effects Do the coding itself

I think this is the preferred order of work, even though it will eventually get mixed up as the idea grows. This way of doing the mod works for me as it keeps me motivated, seeing the creatures and the way they move, sound and behave makes me want to continue non stop.

During the process of coding I like to divide it into little objectives, and developing/testing one by one, to have a sense of advancement and fixing errors in an easier manner.

When seeking for information I recommend using the main page of whichever application you are using to develop the mod, it usually provides clear information or posts about people with the same issues.

And I think that would be all, I know it's a little short but it shows the main problems, tips and strategies I like to use when developing a mod. If you have any doubt, tips or offerings for group mods to make, I'm all ears, contact me through my mail or LinkedIn profiles and i will answer as soon as i can, goodbye
