Whats up with game comunities?

November 8 2025

Hello everyone, today i want to talk about the toxicity of some game comunities.

What is it that people are never happy? I´m about to talk about my personal experience during the HaloCon 2025 and the reveal of the new upcoming game in the franchise, and the horrible acting of the community present.

So, I decided to connect to the twitch HaloCon 2025 with the intention of viewing the trailer for the upcoming game: Halo - Campaign Evolved, so as soon as i could i joined the stream, and decided to wait and have it as background while i worked in university work. Initially the stream started as a tournament, as it was the main intention of the stream, and it was here, when I began to notice the awful situation of the chat surrounding the stream.

The thing that least pissed me off was the fact that a LOT of viewers were just spamming that the drops weren't working properly, and as far as i was concerned, I didn't know the stream had drops at all until they mentioned it, it seems a lot of viewers were here for the drop only, and even though i didnt like it, i was no one to blame them for it, after all i have been the same for other games in the past.

Other than the drops, there were literally hundreds of viewers just whining about the time and that they should be cutting everything and hurrying up to the point. In case it wasn't clear before, the stream was supposed to be MOSTLY A TOURNAMENT, and tournaments can't take an exact amount of time, mostly because of tiebreakers and stuff, but people did not care, apparently they were not aware that the stream was a tournament, and wanted the trailer to come right up to their faces as soon as they showed up, that pissed me off, mainly cause i was actually enjoying the stream till i saw the chat.

Apparently, before the trailer came up to the stream it got leaked in youtube, i decided not to see it, as i wanted to see the big reveal itself, but the chat started again: “GuyS YoU Can LeaVE Now The TrAilEr GoT LeAked” and i mean, if thats that then just leave the god damn stream and leave the ones who actually were there for the stream to enjoy the process, but no, they just spammed about it and nothing else.

And then the reveal showed up, and i absolutely loved it, but people in the halo community just have 2 sides for everything, its either: “Oh Its Not ThE sAmE GAmE aS 40 YeaRs AgO? ThiS nEw GaMe SuckS!” or “Oh I WanT It tO Be LiKe COD aNd OnLy Care AbOut MulTiPlayEr”, jesus, i swear i saw people complaining about the option of sprinting, or even about how ONE OF THE GAME WEAPONS was TO THIN. At least some people were with me, and actually commented about how this game was great, and the community was either 8 year olds that want no campaign or 40 year olds that are crying about the use of sprint in a 40 year old game remaster.

After that i realized most game communities are like this, i mean, minecraft, FNF, And now Halo? Why can't people do nothing but complain? Anyway, that was frustrating, but as I reviewed online there are actually a lot of people who just enjoy the upcoming game, and the communities have a little of both, so that's a little comforting.
